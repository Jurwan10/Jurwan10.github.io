## 🏆 Achievements
- 🎯 **Top Vulnerability Hunter**<br>
    Won a cashprize for **Most Vulnerabilities Reported** at *TEST.FRL*,<br>
    a real-world Red Team exercise hosted by the municipality of De Fryske Marren.

    The event was aimed at reinforcing the overall security posture of the municipality and as a learning exercise for students.
    > This experience solidified my ability to think like an attacker and report and communicate findings in a professional manner.
- 🎯 **3rd & 1st Prize for Most Impactful Hack**<br>
    I attended *HACK.FRL*, the larger follow-up event to *TEST.FRL* in the same concept.<br>
    The event hosted at NHL-Leeuwarden was oriented towards hacking parts of company objects/devices and web domains owned by participating Frisian municipalities, regional water authorities among others.<br><br>
    It gave a large group of students the chance to test our knowledge and find vulnerabilities, work within a scope aswell as correctly reporting findings.<br>
    Doing so with a competetive edge...!<br>
    There were multiple prizes to win in categories like "Most Creative Hack", "Best Documented Hack" and "Most Impactful Hack".<br><br>

    Together with a classmate we tried our best and were able to discover and report multiple vulnerabilities.<br>
    Doing so landed us a shared 3rd & 1st place in the category of "Most Impactful Hack"!🏆<br><br>
    Here i won a Flipper Zero with a case and WiFi Dev-Board!<br>
    Not only this, all winners across all categories were invited to visit the Cyber-Security Centre of the Dutch Defense Department.<br>
    > The event gave a great opportunity to meet and share knowledge with likeminded hackers.
